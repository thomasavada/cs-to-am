## What this project is

Design a path so Joy **CS** can become **AM**.

Not: memorise Joy settings (they already set up the app).
Not: become business owners or media buyers.
Yes: understand the **merchant as a business**, so they can hear the problem behind the ticket.

- They cannot build a real store, so they have never made a merchant's choices.

Thomas, paraphrased — keep the intent:

> **how to launch a store, not a development store.**

> Learn by picking a brand and breaking it down — not theory and remembers.

## What is locked

**Frame**
- Joy is SaaS = **service**. Not an App Store app.
- **Apps are not apps, they are solutions.** Recharge is not a subscription app, it is a lifetime-value
  solution. Klaviyo is a cheap way to reach people at scale, plus a CRM.
- **AI took the execution.** What is left for people is the **outcome**. CS → AM is not a promotion path,
  it is where the work went. This is the talk's opening and the strongest argument in the deck.

**Method**
- **Break down real brands.** Every session is a rep of `6-teardown.md`, not a lecture.
- **Outside-in rule:** if you cannot see it on the public site without a login, it is not in the course.
  This is the guard that keeps ads / funnel / ops / stack in scope and Chase out. It has held four times.
- Textbook = real tickets and real brands. Never invented examples.
- Real launched store, **max 3 apps, to standard**. The store is the *cause* being fixed, not a lab.

**Sequencing**
- Talk → build track → 4 reps → graduation → block 2 (Joy).
- Joy stays out until block 2. Even there: **never answer "how do I turn it on" before "should it be on."**
- A loyalty program is a **multiplier on an existing base**. "Not yet" is a correct answer.

**Locked examples — do not swap**
- **Adidas / the $100 shirt** — price layers and distribution (rep 1)
- **Lumi** — one moisturizer, $42, refill ~8 weeks, customer **Mai**, ~$30 to acquire (rep 1 + journey)
- **Dollar Shave Club** — cutting layers; subscription and referral as answers to a pricing problem
- **HexClad** — the brand that should *not* run points
- **Rae Wellness** — the textbook ICP case: Recharge + Klaviyo, `/rewards` 404s
