# The conversation, replaced

**Used in:** Block 2 — the pass bar

---


**Never:**

> "Do you want store credit or points? Okay, I'll show you where to turn it on."

**Always — in this order:**

1. **Stage** — do they have a base to sell back to, or are they still building one?
2. **Base** — how many customers, how many come back, what is a typical basket?
3. **Fear** — are they losing new people, or over-discounting people who would have paid?
4. **Mechanism** — referral to grow the base, points to build a habit, credit for clarity, tiers to stop blanket discounts
5. **Numbers** — thresholds from their distribution, defended
6. **Placement** — from their journey, not from the demo store
7. **On-brand** — because the currency has to belong to them

Only step 7 onwards is a screen. Steps 1–5 are the service.

---

## Pass bar for block 2

Given a real merchant and a real request:

1. Say whether they should run this **at all**, and why — including "not yet"
2. Choose points / credit / tiers / referral and say **what the other option loses**
3. Produce a tier threshold from their data and defend the number
4. Place the touchpoints from their journey
5. Say why on-brand matters, in one sentence, without using the word "nice"
