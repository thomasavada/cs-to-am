# Where it lives — placement from the journey

**Used in:** Block 2
**See also:** `../journey/3-stops-1-9.md`

---


This is why placement is guesswork today: they put things on a development store where nobody walks.
Real shops have a path — reps 2 and 3 taught it. Now use it.

| Where the customer is | What belongs there | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Product page | "earn 42 points with this" | makes the reward concrete **at the moment of deciding** |
| Cart / checkout | redeem balance | careful — a clumsy redeem step **kills conversion** |
| Thank-you page | "you just earned 42" | the happiest moment they will ever have with you |
| Account page | balance, tier, progress | where a member comes to feel like a member |
| Loyalty page | explain the whole program | the link you can put in email, nav, and a support reply |
| Email (Klaviyo) | balance + what it is worth | **where the balance is actually seen** — most people never log in |

So the questions the merchant asks — *do I need a loyalty page? a widget? where do I show the coupon?
earn or redeem? the profile?* — all have the same answer:

> **Where does this shop's traffic actually go, and where is the customer at that moment?**

**Drill:** for a real brand, place four things and justify each by the journey. Wrong placement, defended
from the journey, beats right placement guessed.
