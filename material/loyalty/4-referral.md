# Referral — the opposite machine

**Used in:** Session 3, beat 6 · Block 2
**See also:** `../cases/dollar-shave-club.md`

---


CS today knows referral as *"turn on the link, share to a friend."* That is the button, not the mechanism.

> **Referral expands the base. Loyalty monetises the base.** Opposite jobs.

Which is exactly why the survival-stage shop in 5.1 — the one that does **not** need points — often
*does* need referral. It is acquisition that does not cost ad money.

Why it works, mechanically:
- The referrer is already a happy customer — the cheapest, most trusted channel there is
- A referred customer arrives with **trust pre-loaded**, so they convert better and stay longer
- It is paid **only on success**, unlike an ad, which is paid on hope

**Case studies — teach by story, not by feature:**

| Brand | The mechanism to actually understand |
|---|---|
| **Dollar Shave Club** | subscription as the model — the next box is already agreed, so retention is structural, not begged for |
| **HexClad** | high-consideration cookware — few natural repeats, so the work goes into referral, community and range expansion, not points |

The lesson from HexClad is the uncomfortable one: **some great brands should not run a points program.**
If CS cannot say that out loud, they are selling, not advising.
