# VIP tiers, and where the number comes from

**Used in:** Block 2

---


**Why a tier program exists at all:** because otherwise you hand the same coupon to a first-time buyer
and to someone who spends $2,000 a year. That is **owner fear #2** — paying people who would have paid anyway.
Tiers are how you stop discounting everyone equally.

**Then the real question: why is the threshold that number?**

Today: CS picks a number, or accepts whatever the merchant guessed. Nobody can defend it.

**The method — from their actual data, not from feeling:**

1. Pull customers with total spend, last 12 months
2. Sort descending
3. Decide what share belongs in each tier — commonly ~5% top, ~20% middle
4. The spend at that cut line **is** your threshold
5. Sanity-check both ways:
   - Is the top tier big enough to be worth running?
   - Is the next tier **reachable** — can a normal customer actually get there in a year?

A threshold nobody can reach is decoration. A threshold everybody clears is a discount for everyone,
which is fear #2 again with extra steps.

> If you cannot say why the number is that number, do not set it.

Also required before you touch tiers — you already have these from `7-shop-map.md`:
orders · typical basket · **how many of 100 buyers come back**.

**Drill:** given a real spend distribution, set three tiers and defend each number out loud.
