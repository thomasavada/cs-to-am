# The product comes first

**Used in:** Session 2, before any channel or mechanism. Echoed in Session 3's "not yet".

---

Every mechanism we teach — email, SMS, WhatsApp, retargeting, subscription, points, tiers, referral —
silently assumes one thing: **that the product earns a second purchase.**

If it does not, none of them work. Points on a product she did not like just means you paid her to be
disappointed twice.

> **A loyalty program is a multiplier.**
> Multiply a small base and it is still small.
> Multiply a **bad product** and it is still **nothing.**

That is the same argument as Shop A vs Shop B, applied to quality instead of size — and it is the
honest first answer that almost nobody gives, because it is not sellable.

---

## Six reasons she does not come back — check them in this order

| | Reason | Where it is fixed |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **The product is not good enough** — she used it, was not impressed | fix the product. Nothing else works. |
| **2** | **She never actually used it** — it is in a drawer | onboarding, the "how to use it" email |
| **3** | **There is no natural repeat** — a mattress, even a great one | accessories, range, referral — not points |
| **4** | **She forgot** — she would buy again, it never crossed her mind | email, SMS, the week-6 reminder |
| **5** | **No reason to choose *you*** — she is buying, from somebody | **this is where we live** |
| **6** | **She would, but it is a hassle** | subscription — agree the next one in advance |

**Everything above rung 5 has to be true before our product does anything at all.**

Work down the list with a real merchant and you will often stop above rung five. That is not bad news.
It is the most useful thing you can tell them, and it is the thing a salesperson will not say.

---

## Checking rung 1 from outside — reviews, but read the complaints

Not the star rating. The **one- and two-star text**, and the recent ones.

| What the complaints are about | What it means |
|---|---|
| shipping, packaging, support, delivery time | the product is fine — **the operation** is leaking. Rungs 2–6 are live options. |
| the product itself — *"didn't work for me"*, *"not worth the price"*, *"broke"* | **retention work is premature.** Say so. |

Reviews are the only honest thing on a product page, and they are **public** — so you can answer
*"is this product worth coming back to?"* in three minutes, for free, before anybody asks you about points.

Delivering this is uncomfortable: you are telling a merchant their product is the problem. Do it anyway,
gently, with the reviews on screen — so it is their own customers saying it, not you.

## Why this matters for the "not yet" conversation

`1-does-it-need-loyalty.md` frames "not yet" as a **stage** problem: not enough base. That is only one
of two causes, and they need opposite answers:

| Cause | Answer | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **No base yet** | referral, acquisition — come back in six months | a **timing** answer |
| **No reason to return** | fix the product | a **product** answer |

**Do not give the timing answer to a product problem.** They will come back in six months with the same
base and the same reviews, and everybody will have wasted a year.
