# On-brand is the mechanism, not decoration

**Used in:** Block 2
**See also:** `2-points-vs-credit.md`

---


They cannot answer *"why does it need to be on brand?"* Here is the answer:

> You chose points **because they are brand currency**. That was the whole advantage over store credit.
> If the widget looks like a generic app bolted onto the site, it is not brand currency any more —
> it is a coupon machine. You threw away the only reason you picked points.

Store credit does not need to be on brand. It is money; money looks like money.
**Points only work if they feel like they belong to that brand.** Name, colour, type, tone.

That is why "make it on brand" is not a cosmetic request from a fussy merchant. It is the difference
between a loyalty program and a discount widget.
