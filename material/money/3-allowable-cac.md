# How much you are allowed to spend on a customer

**Used in:** Session 1, beat 4

---


This is the number that runs every ecom business, and it comes straight out of the tables above.

**Before ads, order 1 keeps:** $51 − 30 − 6 − 1.78 = **$13.22**

- If you only ever get **one** order from her, **$13.22 is your ceiling.** Spend more, you lose.
- If the average customer buys **three** times: $13.22 + $21.96 + $21.96 ≈ **$57**
- Now you can spend **$30, even $40** to acquire her and still win comfortably.

> **How long a customer stays decides how much you are allowed to pay for her.**

Same product. Same ad. Same market. The brand that gets a second order can outspend the one that does not —
**and that is the entire reason retention is a growth strategy and not a nice-to-have.**
