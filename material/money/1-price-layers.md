# Price is layers

**Used in:** Session 1, beat 2
**See also:** `2-shirt-drill.md`

---


Ask the room. Let them guess. The answers will be "brand" and "quality." Both mostly wrong.

**It is distribution.** Price is not the cost of the thing. Price is a **stack of layers, and every layer is
somebody who has to eat.**

| Layer | Pays for | Roughly |
|---|---|---|
| Factory | fabric, labour | **$30** |
| Brand | design, marketing, warehouse | sells it on at ~$60 |
| Distributor / wholesale | moving it, holding stock | ~$75 |
| Retail shop | rent, staff, the shelf it sits on | **$100 to you** |
| Outlet, next season | clearing dead stock | $60 — still above what the shop paid |

The same shoe: **~$25–30 to make. ~$90 to the shop. $180 to you. $108 at the outlet, and still profitable.**

Two things follow, and both are the whole point:

> **The further the product travels from the factory, the more mouths it has to feed.**
>
> **A discount does not eat the profit. It eats the layer that was paying for everything else.**

**DTC exists to delete layers.** Skip wholesale and retail, sell straight from brand to person — that is
why a DTC brand can charge less and still keep more.

But nothing is free. **They deleted the shop and bought an ad instead.** The ad is the new middleman,
and unlike a shop, you pay it again for every single customer.
