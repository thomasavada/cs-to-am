# Apps are not apps, they are solutions

**Used in:** Session 4, beat 1
**See also:** `../journey/7-stack-confession.md`

---


The reframe that carries into rep 4.

> Before you look at Recharge — *is it a subscription app?* **No.**
> It is a solution for **increasing lifetime value.**

| What CS sees | What it actually is | The problem it was bought to fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Recharge / Skio / Appstle** | a **lifetime value** solution | "customers buy once and disappear" |
| **Klaviyo** | a **cheap way to talk to people at scale** — far cheaper than SMS, so you can send to everyone — **plus a CRM**: attributes, birthday, order history, relationship | "I have no way to bring anyone back" |
| **Attentive / Postscript** | SMS — expensive per message, so reserved for urgent moments | "email is not enough for launches" |
| **Rebuy / bundle apps** | a way to **squeeze one transaction** | "there may never be a second order" |
| **Popup tools** | turn **anonymous into known** | "I don't know who is on my site" |
| **Okendo / Judge.me** | **borrowed trust** from strangers | "nobody trusts me yet" |
| **Gorgias / Wonderment** | anxiety management after the order | "I'm drowning in *where is my order*" |
| **Joy** | a **reason to choose us next time** | "I have a base and nothing brings them back" |

Say the sentence, and mean it:

> **We do not sell an app. We sell the solution the app happens to be made of.**

This is the same read as *the stack is a confession* in [4-journey.md](4-journey.md) — from the other side.
There you look at what they installed and infer the fear. Here you learn what each thing is actually **for**.
