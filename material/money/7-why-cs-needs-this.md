# Why CS needs price structure first

**Used in:** Background for the trainer

---


Without price structure, everything a merchant does looks arbitrary and slightly annoying:

- *Why do they care so much about free shipping thresholds?* → it is the cheapest way to raise AOV
- *Why do they push subscriptions so hard?* → the next order is the only profitable one
- *Why are they scared of discounts?* → the discount eats the layer that pays for everything
- *Why do they want referral?* → acquisition that is not an auction they can lose
- *Why do they want a loyalty program?* → they finally have a base worth selling back to
- *Why do they want it live this week?* → every week without it, order 2 is not happening

With it, you can **predict what a merchant will ask for before they ask.** That is the AM read, and it
starts with a $30 shirt.

---

## The drill

1. **Price a shirt.** Cost $30. Set a price and defend it. Then build the order-1 table and find out what you kept.
2. **Find your ceiling.** What is the most you could pay to acquire a customer? Now assume three orders. What is it now?
3. **Classify five real brands** — bought once, or bought again? Then predict what is in their stack, *before* you look.
4. **Then look.** Score yourself.
