## Why it is the textbook

It is the pattern named in Joy's own ICP, standing still:

> **Recharge or Appstle + Klaviyo + no loyalty app.**

They already pay for repeat revenue. They already pay to reach people. And there is **nothing giving a
customer a reason to choose them next time** beyond the subscription itself.

## The distinction it teaches

**Subscription is not loyalty.** A standing order means the next box is already agreed. It does not mean
the customer chose you — it means they have not yet cancelled. Those are different, and the gap between
them is exactly what a loyalty layer is for.

Which is why this is an easy conversation and not a rip-and-replace:

> "You already run Recharge — that is recurring revenue. Loyalty is the missing owned layer:
> points, VIP, referrals, in Klaviyo and in the account. It sits on top of your subscription stack."
