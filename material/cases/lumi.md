# Lumi — the house example

**Used in:** Sessions 1, 2, 3. **Locked — do not swap.**

---

One product. **A moisturizer, $42.** Refill roughly every **8 weeks**.
Customer: **Mai**. Lumi paid about **$30** in ads to reach her.

Small on purpose. Everyone in the room can hold the whole business in their head, which means every
number we add lands on something they already understand.

## Why this shape

| Choice | Why |
|---|---|
| One product | no catalogue to explain, no variants to argue about |
| $42 | big enough that shipping and discounts visibly matter |
| 8-week refill | a real, natural reason to buy again — so retention is possible, not theoretical |
| $30 to acquire | close to the order value, so order 1 is genuinely thin |
| One named customer | *Mai* — a person, not a cohort. You can ask "what is Mai doing right now?" |

## Where Lumi appears

- **Session 1** — after the shirt drill, as the DTC version of the same arithmetic
- **Session 2** — Mai walks stops 1–9 and abandons her cart
- **Session 3** — Mai waits, gets the emails, and reorders at week 8
