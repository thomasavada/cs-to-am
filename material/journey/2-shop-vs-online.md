# A shop vs online — you know who they are

**Used in:** Session 2, beat 2

---


This is the difference between a shop and a website, and nearly every app in ecom exists because of it.

| | **A physical shop** | **An online shop** |
|---|---|---|
| Someone walks in | you see a body, not a person | you know they came from that TikTok ad |
| They browse | you have no record | you know they viewed the moisturizer three times |
| They leave without buying | **gone forever, invisible** | they are on a list |
| You learn who they are | only at the till, only if they pay by card | **at the popup, before they buy anything** |
| You want to talk to them tomorrow | you cannot | email, SMS, or a retargeted ad |

> **In a shop, someone who leaves is gone. Online, someone who leaves is a list.**

That single asymmetry is why the popup exists (turn anonymous into known), why the pixel exists
(see what they did), why retargeting exists (reach the ones who left), and why abandoned-cart email is the
most profitable email in ecom.

It is also why **loyalty works at all**. A loyalty program is identity applied over time — you have to know
that this order and that order came from the same person. A physical shop needs a plastic card for that.
Online, it is just an account.
