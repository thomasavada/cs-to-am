# The stack is a confession

**Used in:** Session 4, beat 2
**See also:** `../money/5-apps-are-solutions.md`

---


Now read it backwards — this is what makes rep 4 work.

Every app a merchant installed is money they spent because they were **worried about something.**

| They installed | So they believe their problem is | Stop |
|---|---|---|
| Klaviyo / Attentive | "I do not know who my visitors are, and I cannot reach them again" | 3, 7, 11 |
| A popup tool | "Too many people leave anonymous" | 3 |
| Okendo / Judge.me | "Strangers do not trust me yet" | 4 |
| Rebuy / bundle app | "My orders are too thin" — **AOV** | 5–6 |
| Recharge / Skio / Appstle | "Customers buy once" — **LTV** | 5, 14 |
| A pixel / tracking tool | "I cannot see what is happening or retarget" | 1, 12 |
| Gorgias / Wonderment | "I am drowning in *where is my order*" | 10 |
| **A loyalty app** | "I have a base and nothing brings them back" | 14 |
| **Nothing at all** | either very early, or nobody is minding the shop | — |

> **The stack tells you what the owner is afraid of, before they say a word.**

That is the AM read. You open a merchant's site, look at what they are paying for, and you already know
what keeps them up at night — and whether the thing they are asking you for is aimed at the right leak.

**And the reverse is the sharpest question in the job:**

> They installed a bundle app but their real leak is that nobody comes back.
> They are fixing stop 6 while bleeding at stop 11. Do you tell them?

Yes. That is the service.
