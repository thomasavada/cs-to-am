# Every stop is a leak, and every leak has a name

**Used in:** Session 2, beat 9

---


The numbers merchants throw at you are not abstract. **Each one is a stop.**

| The number | Which stop | What a low one means | What people buy to fix it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Site speed | 2 | she left before the page even loaded | theme / image work |
| Bounce rate | 2 | the ad promised something the page did not | landing page that matches the ad |
| **Email capture rate** | 3 | you do not know who is visiting | **popup** — Klaviyo, Attentive |
| **Add-to-cart rate** | 4 | the page did not convince her | reviews, photos, clearer offer |
| **Subscription rate** | 5 | no reason to commit to the next one | **subscription app** — Recharge, Skio |
| **Bundle / attach rate** | 5–6 | orders are too thin | **bundle & upsell app** — Rebuy |
| **AOV** | 5–6 | orders are too thin | bundles, upsell, free-shipping threshold |
| Cart abandon | 7 | normal — but only recoverable if you have her email | **email / SMS flows** |
| **Checkout rate** | 8 | shipping shock, friction, forced account | threshold, express wallets, guest checkout |
| **Conversion rate** | 2–8 | *a symptom, not a problem* — find the stop first | depends entirely where the leak is |
| WISMO ticket volume | 10 | she is anxious and nobody told her anything | tracking, proactive shipping email |
| **Return customer rate** | 11–14 | nothing brings her back | lifecycle email, subscription, **loyalty** |
| **LTV** | 11–14 | each customer is worth too little | subscription, loyalty, more products to sell her |

**The discipline this teaches:** *"conversion is down"* is **not a problem.** It is a symptom of a leak at
one specific stop. An AM finds the stop. CS forwards the sentence.
