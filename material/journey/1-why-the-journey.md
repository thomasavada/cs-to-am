# Why you learn the journey

**Used in:** Session 2, beat 1
**See also:** `6-leak-map.md`

---


Not so you can recite fourteen stops.

**Every stop is a place where money leaks out.** Every leak has a name, a number, and a fix.
That is the whole reason this matters:

> You cannot find the opportunity if you cannot see the journey.

A merchant never writes in saying *"I have a leak at stop 6."* They say *"conversion is down"*, or
*"I want store credit"*, or *"rewards aren't working."* Your job is to know **which stop they are standing on** —
and whether the thing they asked for is even the fix.
