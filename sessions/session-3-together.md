## The beat everything builds to
**The 404.** Rae Wellness, cold. Ctrl-F `recharge` — hit. `klaviyo` — hit. `smile`, `loyaltylion`,
`yotpo` — nothing. Then `/pages/rewards` → **404.** Thirty seconds, no login, no discovery call.

## Fit is learned by contrast
Never one brand. Always **two side by side**, one of which fails the checklist. A checklist memorised is
trivia; a checklist run against a brand that fails it is judgement.

## The gate
1. A **cold teardown** a lead accepts — a brand never seen, 15 minutes
2. **8 of 12** timed ticket restatements
3. Your store, **launched, to standard** — max three apps, every one defensible

Then, and only then: Joy on screen — placement and on-brand config, `material/loyalty/5` and `/6`.
