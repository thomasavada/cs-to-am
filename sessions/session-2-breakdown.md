## Live, not screenshots
Walk the real site in front of them. The popup firing and the free-shipping bar moving are worth more
than any slide. Screenshots are the fallback if the wifi dies — see `resources.md`.
**Stop before paying.** Nobody buys anything on the projector.

## Homework
1. A **full** teardown on two more brands
2. Walk your own store on your phone, mark where you'd quit
3. Three tickets — which part of the path is each really about?
