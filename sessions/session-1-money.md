## The one beat that must not be rushed

Build **order 1 on the whiteboard**, line by line, with the room. Do not show the finished table.
They have to feel the $60 disappear. Then stop, and let the silence do the work before order 2.

## Homework

1. §0–1 on **two** more brands
2. Classify each *bought once* or *bought again* — **predict the stack before you look.** Then look. Score yourself.
3. What does one order cost *your* store?
