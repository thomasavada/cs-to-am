## The beat that must not be rushed
**The whiteboard build.** Order 1, line by line, with the room. They have to feel the $60 disappear.
Then stop, and let the silence work before order 2.

## The slide that decides the course
**"What it does for you on Monday."** If they think this is homework for a promotion, they will half-do
the reps. If they believe it makes Monday easier, they will show up.

## Homework

2. Classify each *bought once* / *bought again* — predict the stack before you look, then look
3. What does one order cost *your* store?
