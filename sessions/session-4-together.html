05
A test you can run on yourself, and on any pair reporting back
The “why” chain
“They should run a subscription.”
What for? — “to increase LTV.”
Why does LTV need increasing?
To what number? Why that number?
What do they get there that they do not get now?
Same for AOV
“They need higher AOV.”
How much is low?
How much is enough?
Enough for what?
If the chain breaks anywhere, the business was not understood — no matter how full the sheet is.
Three questions and you find out immediately whether somebody read the business or repeated a word they have heard on calls.

Why

Why not

10
“Not yet” has two causes, and only one of them is about size
No base — or no product worth returning to.
No base yet
A real product, too few people. Fixable, and quickly — referral and acquisition. Come back to us in six months.
No reason to return
Read the one-star reviews. If the complaints are about the product itself, no mechanism we sell will create a second order.
One is a timing answer. The other is a product answer.
Do not give the timing answer to a product problem. They will come back in six months with the same base and the same reviews, and we will have wasted everybody’s year.

13
The conversation, replaced
Never: “Points or store credit? OK, I’ll show you where to turn it on.”
  • 1 Stage — is there a base to sell back to?
  • 2 Base — how many, how many return, typical basket
  • 3 Fear — losing new people, or over-discounting?
  • 4 Mechanism — referral, points, credit, tiers
  • 5 Numbers — thresholds from their data, defended
  • 6 Placement — from their journey, not the demo store
Only step 6 is a screen.
Steps one to five are the service. That is what we are actually paid for.
Remember the talk
AI took the execution. What is left for people is the outcome — judgement about one specific business, and being accountable for it.
