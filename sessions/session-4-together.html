But does this shop need a loyalty program at all?

A loyalty program is a multiplier

Multiply a small number — it is still small.

So the first question is stage

Remember from last week — referral grows the base, loyalty monetises it.

"Not yet" is a correct answer.

Telling a survival-stage shop to launch points is not service. It is selling them the wrong thing, it will not produce a result, and they will churn — correctly.

The hardest thing you will have to say

They installed a bundle app. Their real leak is that nobody comes back.

They are fixing the cart while bleeding at the second order.

Do you tell them?

The conversation, replaced

Never: "Points or store credit? OK, I'll show you where to turn it on."

  • 1 Stage — is there a base to sell back to?
  • 2 Base — how many, how many return, typical basket
  • 3 Fear — losing new people, or over-discounting?
  • 4 Mechanism — referral, points, credit, tiers
  • 5 Numbers — thresholds from their data, defended
  • 6 Placement — from their journey, not the demo store

Only the last step is a screen.

Everything before it is the thing a machine cannot do for them.
