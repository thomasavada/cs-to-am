## The two beats that carry the session

**The inbox.** They bought something in the build track. Those confirmation, shipping and review emails
are in their pocket right now. Put a real one on the screen and walk it. Nothing beats their own order.

**"No reason" is allowed.** When the weak-repeat brand comes up, let the room land on *there is no reason
to buy this twice.* Do not rescue it. That discomfort is the whole setup for session 4.
