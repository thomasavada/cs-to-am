10
The conversation, replaced
Never: “Points or store credit? OK, I’ll show you where to turn it on.”
  • 1 Stage — is there a base to sell back to?
  • 2 Base — how many, how many return, typical basket
  • 3 Fear — losing new people, or over-discounting?
  • 4 Mechanism — referral, points, credit, tiers
  • 5 Numbers — thresholds from their data, defended
  • 6 Placement — from their journey, not the demo store
Only step 6 is a screen.
Steps one to five are the service. That is what we are actually paid for.
Remember the talk
