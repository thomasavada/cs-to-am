09
So where you are heading
Four questions you can answer about any merchant — before opening Joy.
1 · What does this shop sell, and how does it make money on one order?
2 · How do people arrive, and where do they quit?
3 · Why would someone buy a second time — and what is the brand doing about it?
4 · Is it ours, does it need us, and what is the one thing we would change?
If you cannot answer 1–3, you are not allowed to open the app.
