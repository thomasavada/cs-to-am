What is actually being bought here?

The product page answers three questions

Will this work for me?
Can I trust you?
What if I hate it?

The cart, and the strongest lever in ecom

"You are $12 away from free shipping."

She would rather add $12 of product than pay $7 of shipping for nothing.

Checkout — where intent goes to die

  • Extra costs are the #1 abandon reason — 39%
  • The discount code box is a leak
  • Express wallets: five fields → one thumbprint
  • Guest checkout — do not force an account on a stranger

~70% of carts are abandoned. That is normal, not failure.

baymard.com/lists/cart-abandonment-rate
