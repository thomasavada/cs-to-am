Should a shop run a popup?

WHY — yes — it buys her identity

Not the sale — the email. Without it you cannot recover a cart, send a refill reminder, or retarget. Everything after this depends on it.

WHY NOT — no — not like that

Fires instantly, on mobile, before she has seen anything — she bounces. And the 10% goes to people who would have paid full price. Fear #2 in the first thirty seconds.

The product page answers three silent questions

Will this work for me?
Can I trust you?
What if I hate it?

Reviews answer all three — cheaper than any copy you could write.

A hidden returns policy kills the sale. A stranger will not risk $42 on a shop that will not say what happens if it fails.

Subscribe & save, or a bundle?

WHY — subscription — buys LTV

The next order is already agreed. Predictable revenue, and she stops shopping around. Costs ~15% margin, forever.

WHY NOT — bundle — buys AOV

A fatter order today. Right when there may never be a second order. Less margin per unit, but the cash is now.
