## The beat everything builds to

**The 404.** Open Rae Wellness cold. View source, Ctrl-F `recharge` — hit. `klaviyo` — hit.
`smile`, `loyaltylion`, `yotpo`, `rivo` — nothing. Then type `/pages/rewards`. **404.**

Thirty seconds, no login, no discovery call, and they found the entire pitch themselves.
That is the moment the four sessions pay off — let them see it land.

## Fit is learned by contrast

Never one brand. Always **two side by side**, one of which is not ours. A checklist memorised is trivia;
a checklist run against a brand that fails it is judgement.

## Homework — into the graduation

1. §6–8 on two more brands, **one of which should not run loyalty**
2. **Eight tickets:** restatement + at most **one** fork. Two questions on one item = that item fails.
3. Your store: finished to the bar? Max three apps, every one defensible.

## The gate

1. A **cold teardown** a lead accepts — a brand you have never seen, 15 minutes
2. **8 of 12** timed ticket restatements
3. Your store, **launched, to standard**
