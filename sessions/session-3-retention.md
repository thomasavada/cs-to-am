## The two beats that carry it
**Their own inbox.** They bought something in the build track. Those emails are on their phones right now.
Put a real one on screen. Nothing I could write competes with an order they placed themselves.

**"No reason" is allowed.** On the weak-repeat brand, let the room land on *there is no reason to buy this
twice.* Do not rescue it. That discomfort is the entire setup for session 4.
