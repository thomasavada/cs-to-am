## The beat that reframes everything
**The product comes first.** Before any channel or mechanism: *why would she buy it again? Because she
liked it. Did she? Do we know?*

> A loyalty program is a multiplier. Multiply a small base and it is still small.
> Multiply a **bad product** and it is still **nothing**.

Not sellable — which is exactly why the room will trust everything after it.

## "No reason" is allowed
On HexClad, let the room land on *there is no reason to buy this twice*. Do not rescue it.
That discomfort is the entire setup for session 4.
