# The Talk — from app support to retention service

**20–25 minutes, then sit down.** The heading, not the course.
**Deck:** `00-talk.html` · 14 slides · **Outline:** [`../1-talk.md`](../1-talk.md)

Audience: Joy CS. Works for a wider room (leads, management) without changes — nothing in it is
CS-only, and the "why now" half is the company argument.

## Do not flip 2 and 4
The ticket first, then the compare. Opening on the table is abstract; opening on a ticket they
recognise makes the compare land on something real.

## The two beats that carry it
**The Vietnamese line.** Say it yourself, slowly. This is where the room stops feeling accused and
starts feeling explained.

> Không phải vì tiếng Anh. Vì chưa biết shop chạy thế nào, nên câu của merchant không ghim vào đâu được.

**"What it does for you on Monday."** If they hear *promotion track*, they will half-do the reps.
If they believe it makes Monday easier, they show up.

## Close and stop
> We don't get paid because they learned the app. They get paid when the same customer orders again.
> Your job is that outcome — faster.

Sit down. Do not add anything.
