- [ ] **A brand chosen, and walked yourself the night before** — know where its popup fires
- [ ] Meta Ad Library open in a tab with that brand searched — [facebook.com/ads/library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/)

## The live beats

Do **not** screenshot these. Walk the real site in front of them — the popup firing and the free-shipping
bar moving are worth more than any slide. Have screenshots as a fallback only if the wifi dies.

**Stop before paying.** Nobody buys anything on the projector.

## Homework

1. §2–3 on two more brands
2. Walk your own store on your phone. Mark where you'd quit.
3. Three tickets from your queue — for each: **which stop** is it really about?
