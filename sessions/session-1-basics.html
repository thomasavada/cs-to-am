Every layer is somebody who has to eat — rent, staff, a shelf, a warehouse.

The further a product travels from the factory, the more mouths it has to feed.

A discount does not eat the profit. It eats the layer that was paying for everything else.

So should a shop ever discount?

WHY — yes — to win a stranger

She has never heard of you. $9 buys a first order you would not otherwise get, and her email, which is worth more than the $9.

WHY NOT — no — to someone already buying

That same $9 goes to people who were coming anyway. On a $100 shoe adidas keeps $2 — a 10% discount does not shrink that profit, it erases it four times over.
