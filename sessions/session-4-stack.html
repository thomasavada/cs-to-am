"Not yet" is a correct answer.

Telling a survival-stage shop to launch points is not service. It is selling them the wrong thing. It will not produce a result.

And they will churn — correctly.

What you can do now — no app

  • What does this shop sell, and how does it make money on one order?
  • How do people arrive, and where do they quit?
  • Why would someone buy twice — and what is the brand doing about it?
  • Is it ours, does it need this, and what is the one thing we would change?
