## The two beats that must not be rushed
**The whiteboard build.** Order 1, line by line, with the room. They have to feel the $60 disappear.
Then stop, and let the silence work before order 2.

**The view-source demo.** Sixty seconds, and they know more than a discovery call would tell them.
It is the moment the job changes shape for them.
