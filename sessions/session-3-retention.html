Does a loyalty program help this shop?

WHY — Lumi — a refill brand

$42 moisturizer, runs out every 8 weeks. She has to rebuy something — points decide it is Lumi, and decide it is now rather than in three weeks.

WHY NOT — HexClad — premium cookware

A pan is a five-year decision. Give her 400 points and they expire before they are worth anything. You added a widget, a cost and a promise, and produced no second order.

They ask for store credit. Do you just switch it on?

WHY — credit fits — considered, rare, returns

Expensive one-off purchases, or a shop with lots of returns. Credit is understood instantly and keeps the money in the shop instead of refunding it out.

WHY NOT — points fit — refill, habit, membership

Credit is money with your logo on it — transactional, no attachment, and you are holding their cash. Points are the brand’s currency, and they build a habit.

Points or credit — the whole trade

POINTS

the brand’s currency

  • abstract — needs explaining
  • feels like belonging, progress
  • you name it, you theme it
  • some are never redeemed

STORE CREDIT

money with your logo on it

  • feels like a transaction
  • looks like money, not like you
  • spent fast — you hold their cash

Why $500?

Where the number actually comes from

  • Pull customers with total spend, last 12 months
  • Sort, highest first
  • Decide the share per tier — commonly ~5% top, ~20% middle
  • The spend at that cut line is your threshold

too high

nobody reaches it — decoration

too low

everybody clears it — a discount for everyone. Fear #2 with extra steps.

If you cannot say why the number is that number, do not set it.
