Why $500?

Where the number actually comes from

  • Pull customers with total spend, last 12 months
  • Sort, highest first
  • Decide the share per tier — commonly ~5% top, ~20% middle
  • The spend at that cut line is your threshold

Then check both ways: is the top tier big enough to matter, and is the next tier reachable in a year?

If you cannot say why the number is that number, do not set it.
