# The course — four reps

**Supersedes the original four-session draft** (now in `_archive/`). Same container (4 × 2h). Opposite engine.

Old engine: sit in a room, learn boxes, drill tickets.  
New engine (from `questions.md`): **pick a brand, break it down.** Every session is one rep of
`6-teardown.md` plus **one new lens**. Nothing is memorised. The sheet is memorised *by using it*.

**Outside-in rule:** if you cannot see it on the public website without a login, it is not in this course.  
That is what keeps ads / CR / fulfilment / stack in scope and keeps ad-account strategy out.

Still true: **no Joy screens.** Still true: `7-shop-map.md` is the printable that stays open on the table.

## Build track — runs first, runs throughout

Not a lab. `questions.md` names this as the **cause**: they cannot read a merchant because they have
never made a merchant's choices.

**A real launched store. Not a development store.**

The standard matters as much as the store. Today CS stores are *"many apps, low quality, not to standard."*
That is the failure being corrected.

**Bar — a lead should not be able to tell it is a training store:**

- [ ] One product. Real photos. A real reason it exists.
- [ ] A brand: name, one colour, one font, a logo. It reads like a shop, not a demo.
- [ ] Real checkout that takes money (test payments or one cheap live SKU)
- [ ] Shipping rate set on purpose, with a threshold you chose and can defend
- [ ] Policies written: returns, shipping, contact
- [ ] **Max 3 apps.** Every app must survive the question *"what job does this do?"*
- [ ] One way a customer arrives (a link you send yourself counts)
- [ ] One reason to come back

**The exercise:** complete **order 1**, then attempt **order 2** as the same customer.  
Report what it cost you, and what would make you buy again.

Checkpoint at the top of every session: 10 min, screens on the wall.

## The four sessions

Each session is **one topic with one goal**, and each is a rep of the teardown sheet, not a lecture.
Decks and runsheets: [`sessions/`](sessions/).

| | Session | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **How a shop makes money** | a shop is a business, not a website |
| **2** | **What is wrong with this shop?** | find the real problem, and say which to fix first |
| **3** | **Why people come back** | why someone buys twice, and what actually makes them |
| **4** | **Bring it together** | is it ours, does it need us, what do we say |

The arc is **model → diagnosis → domain → judgement.** Session 1 gives them the mental model.
Session 2 makes them a troubleshooter. Session 3 gives them our subject. Session 4 makes them decide.

**Shape of every session — 2 hours:**

| | |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Store checkpoint, homework on the table |
| 0:10 | **Teach — slides, with live-site beats woven through** |
| 1:00 | **Rep — a real brand, in pairs, 45 min** |
| 1:45 | Report out, every pair |

If the teaching ever crowds the rep out, it is eating the thing that actually works.


## The ticket track — homework, then the gate

Tickets do not get their own session any more. They ride along, because a ticket from a brand you have
torn down lands somewhere, and a ticket from a stranger does not.

- **Every teardown ends at section 8:** what would this brand email us about? Which box, which fear.
- **Between reps:** restate real tickets from your own queue. Box + fear + 2 sentences in your own words.
  Vietnamese is fine. **No questions to the merchant.**
- **Banned all the way through:** "Could you please provide more details?" / "Can you explain more?"
- **Allowed:** restate first, then **one** fork that proves you already understood 80%.
- Forward template (`7-shop-map.md`): you may not forward if **I think** is empty.

**Graduation — all three, not two:**

1. A teardown a lead accepts on a brand they have never seen — cold, in 15 minutes
2. **8 of 12** timed ticket restatements a lead would act on
3. A launched store that meets the bar above

**Not yet** is a normal outcome. It means another stack of reps next week, not another lecture.

## Textbook

Real brands only. Mix of:

- **Live Joy merchants** — the teardown output is immediately useful to the team
- **Strong-ICP brands** (Halfdays, Dieux, Crown Affair, Hostage Tape, Rae Wellness)
- **Deliberate non-fits** — so ICP is learned by contrast
- **Their own store** — the only one where they made the choices

Plus 30 anonymised ticket subjects + 15 threads for the ticket track. Still needed before rep 1.

## After the four — block 2

Only then: **[5-joy-logic.md](5-joy-logic.md)** — two more reps where Joy is finally on screen.

Not a toggle tour. Points vs store credit and *why*. VIP thresholds from real data. Referral as the
opposite machine. Where touchpoints go, read off the customer journey. Why on-brand is the mechanism,
not decoration.

Its one rule: **never answer "how do I turn it on" until you have answered "should it be on."**

Do not open block 2 until the graduation bar above has been run at least once.
