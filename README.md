## Locked

**What we sell**
- Joy is **SaaS = service**: ecom + retention expertise. Not an App Store app.
- Merchants are a **business**. They want to **earn more**, not spend less, not learn the app.
- Time-to-result is the product: set up fast → see a result fast.
- **Apps are not apps, they are solutions.** Recharge is not a subscription app, it is a lifetime-value
  solution. We sell the solution the app is made of.

**Why now**
- **AI took the execution.** "Where do I click" is what machines do best — instantly, in perfect English.
  What is left for people is the **outcome**: judgement about one business, accountability over time.
  CS → AM is not a promotion path, it is where the work went.
- Which kills the English excuse twice: it is the wrong diagnosis *and* the worst thing to compete on.

**The failure being fixed**
- CS answers the app. When they don't understand, they ask the merchant to explain until they can
  copy-forward. Looks like English. It is **no ecom picture**.
- They cannot build a real store, so they have never made a merchant's choices.

**How we teach**
- **Break down real brands, not remember.** Memorising does not stick without understanding.
- **Outside-in rule:** if you cannot see it on the public site without a login, it is not in the course.
  Keeps ads / funnel / ops / stack in, keeps ad-account strategy out.
- Textbook is **their real tickets** and **real brands** — never fake examples.
- Learn on a **real launched store**, max 3 apps, to standard. Today's CS stores are app-soup.

**Sequencing**
- A loyalty program is a **multiplier on an existing base**, not a growth engine. "Not yet" is a correct answer.
- Joy comes last, and even then: **never answer "how do I turn it on" before "should it be on."**
